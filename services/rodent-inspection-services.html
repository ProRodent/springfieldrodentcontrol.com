Rodent Inspection Services in Springfield, MO

An inspection finds where rodents get in, where they live and what has to change. The written quote follows from that.

Call (844) 635-0403 Contact Us

Every job starts with an inspection. A good one answers three questions: which animals, how many routes in and what conditions are helping them.

What an inspector looks for

University of Missouri Extension lists the signs: droppings, fresh gnawing, tracks, shredded nests, dark rub marks and a musky odor. We read those signs against the building to decide where to put traps and which gaps to seal.

Inside and out

Outside: foundation, vents, utility lines, garage doors, burrows and yard conditions. Inside: attic, crawl space or basement, kitchen, garage and utility rooms. Each location is photographed or noted for the written summary.

How the quote is worked out

The free written quote reflects property size, the number of entry points, the species, how long the problem has been present and whether cleanup or insulation work is needed. We give it in writing before work starts. We do not post prices online because every property is different.

Free diagnostics and second opinions

Some callers want a diagnostic before committing to anything, or a second opinion and a more broken-down quote after another company or a buyer's inspector flagged rodents. We inspect the areas named in that report, explain what we find in plain language and give a written quote. Bring the report if you have one.

Inspection covers

  • Exterior walk-around and gap check
  • Attic, crawl space and basement inspection
  • Kitchen, pantry and garage check
  • Notes on food, water and shelter sources
  • Written findings and free quote

FAQs About Rodent Inspection Services in Springfield

Is the inspection free?

We offer a free inspection and a free written quote. Call to book.

How long does it take?

Most home inspections take about an hour, depending on property size and access.

What if you find nothing?

We tell you. Not every noise is a rodent, and we will not sell work that is not needed.

Do I have to be home?

It helps, especially for access to the attic and crawl space, but arrangements can often be made.

Can you tell me what kind of pest this is?

That is part of the inspection. Droppings, gnawing, tracks and nests tell us whether it is a mouse, a rat or something else.

Can you give a second opinion on another quote?

Yes. Share what the other company listed and we will inspect those items and explain in writing what each involves.
