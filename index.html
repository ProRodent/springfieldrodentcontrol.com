Signs of Rats and Mice in a Building

University of Missouri Extension lists droppings, fresh gnawing, tracks, nests made of shredded material, dark rub marks along walls and a musky odor as signs of rodents. Mouse droppings look like dark pieces of rice, and Norway rat droppings are larger. You may also hear scratching in walls at night.

Size matters when it comes to entry points. The same source says mice can enter openings a little wider than one quarter inch, and rats can gnaw openings of about half an inch. If you find a gap that size around a pipe, vent or door, it is worth closing, and you can read how we do it on our rodent exclusion page.

Source: University of Missouri Extension, Prevent mice and rats from coming inside.

What University of Missouri Extension lists as signs

  • Droppings, which look like dark pieces of rice for mice and are larger for Norway rats
  • Fresh gnawing on wood, packaging or wiring
  • Tracks and dark rub marks along walls
  • Nests made of shredded material
  • A musky odor in closed spaces
